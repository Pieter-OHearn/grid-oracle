## Decision update rule

Keep stable IDs. Record date, evidence, alternatives, rationale and affected workplans when changing an adopted/proposed decision. Never turn a proposed option into owner approval silently. A model choice requires an experiment report; a host choice requires measured capacity. Update PLAN's next action after resolving a dependency.
