### Required delivery contract

Follow the established homelab deployment boundary: GridOracle's repository builds and
tests immutable OCI images, releases them to GHCR, and records their version and
digest. The homelab repository owns the GridOracle service manifest and Compose stack;
a reviewed homelab PR promotes a specific released digest. The existing self-hosted
deployer runner deploys only the merged homelab configuration after its normal plan,
risk gate and verification steps. It does not build GridOracle source, train models,
or consume a mutable image tag.

The agent must design and evidence a model-bundle manifest that identifies the model,
training data, feature schema, calibration and artifact hashes. Runtime recovery must
load that declared bundle without access to the training machines. Use Quro's GHCR
image release and digest-pinned homelab stack as the reference pattern; do not copy
its application-specific services or automatic-update mechanism.
