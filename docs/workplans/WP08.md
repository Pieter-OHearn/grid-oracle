## Work completed and artifacts

The reference is a small horizon-specific linear Plackett–Luce ranker with shrunk
identity effects and optional team×circuit table. The challenger adds a shared
small tanh network. Censored entrants remain in the winner denominator but are
not assigned last-place ranking targets. Fixed scales/imputation and train-only
vocabularies prevent evaluation statistics from entering fitted models.

Every contender selects settings on frozen tuning races, refits only allowed
train+tune rows, and temperature-calibrates only the calibration block. All
selection decisions are frozen before evaluation. There is no selector that
publishes a model. [README](../research/wp08/README.md) documents environments,
likelihood, numerical tolerances, interruption/resume and rollback.
